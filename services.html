Care at The Bridge

Three doorways.
One place to begin.

Therapy is not a menu of problems. These are simply three ways into a conversation that is shaped around the people in the room.

You can be unsure which kind of support you need. That is allowed.
01 — For me

When something inside you needs room.

Individual therapy creates a private place to work through anxiety, stress, grief, identity, self-esteem, difficult patterns, major transitions or simply the sense that something is no longer working.

Begin individual intake ↗
02 — For us

When love is still there,
but understanding isn't.

Couples therapy slows the pattern down. It creates room to hear what is underneath the conflict, address damaged trust and find healthier ways to relate without turning the session into a courtroom.

Begin couples intake ↗
03 — For family

When being connected no longer feels like being close.

Family therapy helps people navigate conflict, transition and distance while making room for every voice — including the quietest one.

Begin family intake ↗
Not sure?

You do not need to diagnose yourself before you arrive.

Start the private intake and choose “I'm not sure yet.” The first conversation can help clarify an appropriate starting point.

Begin privately ↗
